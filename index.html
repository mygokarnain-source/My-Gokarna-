Off The Beaten Path

Hidden Gems & Gokarna Sightseeing

Beyond the beaches — waterfalls, caves, temples & islands most tourists miss.

gem1.jpg

Yana Caves

Giant limestone rock formations deep in the forest — best explored early morning to avoid heat and crowds.

gem2.jpg

Murudeshwar Temple

Home to one of the tallest Shiva statues in the world, set right on the coast with sweeping sea views.

gem3.jpg

Vibhooti Falls

A hidden monsoon waterfall on the way to Yana — best visited between July and October.

gem4.jpg

Mirjan Fort

A 16th-century laterite fort surrounded by lush greenery — great for photography and history lovers.

gem5.jpg

Netrani Island

Karnataka's heart-shaped island getaway, known for coral reefs and scuba diving off the coast.

gem6.jpg

Sunset Point

A cliffside viewpoint between Kudle and Om Beach offering Gokarna's most magical sunset views.
