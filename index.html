🗓️ Gokarna Trip Plans

1-Day Gokarna Trip Plan

If you reach Gokarna early in the morning, first visit the Mahabaleshwar Temple and Maha Ganapati Temple. Then rent a bike and explore some of Gokarna’s hidden places, such as Shiva Cave, Jatayu Teertha, Ram Teertha, and Koti Teertha.

In the evening, you can explore the beautiful beaches—Om Beach, Kudle Beach, Paradise Beach, and Half Moon Beach—and complete your 1-day trip.

Note: But according to me, it’s better to plan a 2-day or 3-day trip to Gokarna so you can explore the places properly and enjoy the trip without rushing.

2-Day Gokarna Trip Plan

If you’re planning a trip to Gokarna on Saturday and Sunday, here’s a plan you can follow:

Day 1 – Saturday

If you reach Gokarna around 7:00 AM, check in to your stay, freshen up, and first visit the Gokarna temples for darshan.

After that, rent a bike and head straight to Mirjan Fort. By the time you finish exploring, it will be around lunchtime, so have lunch there.

Then, travel to Honnavar and spend around 2 hours exploring places like Manki Beach, Eco Beach, and other nearby attractions.

Around 5:00 PM, leave for Murudeshwar. Visit the temple, have darshan, and enjoy the beach experience.

Later at night, return to Gokarna, enjoy the nightlife and party, and complete Day 1.

Day 2 – Sunday

Start around 7:00 AM and head straight to Yana Caves. On the way back, you can also visit Vibhuti Falls.

By the time you return, it will be around lunchtime. After lunch, spend the evening exploring the beautiful places and beaches around Gokarna, such as:

  • Shiva Cave
  • Om Beach
  • Kudle Beach
  • Patanga Beach
  • Paradise Beach
  • Half Moon Beach
  • Belekan Beach
  • Sunset Point

Cover as many places as possible depending on your time and energy.

Finally, return to Gokarna at night and head back to your hometown, completing your 2-day Gokarna trip.

3-Day Gokarna Trip Plan

Day 1 – Gokarna, Mirjan, Honnavar & Murudeshwar

On the first day, start by visiting the Mahabaleshwar Temple in Gokarna and complete your darshan.

After that, rent a bike and head straight to Mirjan Fort. From there, travel to Honnavar and then continue to Murudeshwar.

On the way, you can also visit Apsarakonda Falls and enjoy the beach.

If you’re visiting during the summer season, you can make the trip even more exciting by trying scuba diving at Netrani Island near Murudeshwar.

In Honnavar, you can also enjoy backwater boating through the mangroves, which gives you a Kerala-style boating experience.

Day 2 – Yana & Waterfalls

On the second day, head to Yana Caves.

While returning from Yana, you can explore several beautiful waterfalls, including:

  • Vibhuti Falls
  • Donne Falls
  • Hosadevata Falls

If you’re willing to travel a little farther, you can also visit:

  • Vibhuti Falls
  • Kandagara Falls

You can cover as many waterfalls as possible depending on the time available.

Day 3 – Gokarna Temples, Hidden Gems & Beaches

On the last day, explore the temples around Gokarna and then spend the rest of the day discovering the hidden gems and beautiful beaches around Gokarna.

Places you can cover include:

  • Half Moon Beach
  • Om Beach
  • Kudle Beach
  • Paradise Beach
  • Shiva Cave
  • Belekan Beach
  • Lighthouse
  • Rama Teertha
  • Main Beach

If you have enough time, you can also add some of these places to Day 2.

After completing your trip, return to Gokarna and head back to your hometown at night, completing your 3-day Gokarna trip.
